Resetting a password gives the user a new encryption key: their saved history and personal dictionary are deleted and every device is signed out. That is what keeps personal data unreadable to administrators.

Terms are offered to the recogniser as hints, replace their "sounds like" variants after recognition, and are kept exactly by the cleanup step. Users' own dictionaries take precedence.
